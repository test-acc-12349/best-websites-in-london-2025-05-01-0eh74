Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks. Simple websites can be done in 2-3 weeks, while more complex e-commerce sites might take 8-10 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What's included in your website maintenance packages?

Our maintenance packages include regular updates, security monitoring, daily backups, performance optimization, and technical support. We also offer content updates and SEO monitoring in our premium packages.

Do you provide hosting services?

Yes, we offer premium hosting solutions with 99.9% uptime guarantee, SSL certificates, and CDN integration. Our hosting includes 24/7 monitoring and regular security updates.

Can you help with SEO and digital marketing?

Absolutely! We provide comprehensive SEO services including keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, and content strategy. We also offer PPC management and social media marketing.
